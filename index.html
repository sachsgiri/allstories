In 2177, India is a land of dust storms, water wars, and quantum bureaucracy. A young auditor forced into a service she never chose is quietly recruited by the समिती, the state's oldest secret: a committee that has spent two centuries collecting small black pyramids no science can open. Her assignment leads to a dying village matriarch, a Luddite sect whose call-and-response folk riddles are older than they know, and a chamber beneath a ruined temple where two lovers have been frozen mid-embrace for a thousand years. The deeper she digs, the closer the story comes home: to her mother's kitchen, and a wooden mortar that has been waiting eight hundred years for her.
